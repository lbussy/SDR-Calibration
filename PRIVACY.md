# SDR Calibration Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 20, 2026

This policy applies to the SDR Calibration application distributed through the
Microsoft Store and to project support provided through this repository.

## Information processed by the application

SDR Calibration processes files that you explicitly select or identify,
including calibration requests, recorded complex-IQ observations, reference
registries, trust files, profiles, and output locations. Those files and their
paths may contain personal information or other sensitive information chosen
by you.

Processing occurs locally on your device. The application writes calibration
results and supporting evidence only to a new output directory you select or
specify. It does not require an account and does not include project telemetry,
advertising, analytics, cloud synchronization, or a project-operated network
service. SDR Calibration does not send your selected files, their contents, or
generated results to the project maintainer or any third party.

You control the source files and generated results on your device. You may
review, move, retain, or delete them using your operating system. Uninstalling
the application does not necessarily delete files you created or selected.

## Support and security reports

If you open a GitHub Issue, you choose what information to send to GitHub and
make visible in the public repository. Do not post calibration data, recorded
IQ, credentials, private keys, personal information, or other sensitive
material in a public Issue. GitHub handles information submitted through its
service under GitHub's own privacy terms.

Report security vulnerabilities through the repository's private
[security-reporting process](SECURITY.md). Submit only the information needed
to investigate the report. Retention and disclosure practices for those
reports are described in that security policy and are also subject to GitHub's
service controls.

## Microsoft Store and operating-system data

Microsoft and your operating system may independently process Store account,
installation, diagnostic, crash, or usage information under their own settings
and privacy terms. The SDR Calibration project does not receive such
information unless you deliberately provide it through a support or security
channel.

## Children

SDR Calibration is a technical utility and is not directed to children. The
application does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes to this policy

Material changes will be published in this repository with a revised effective
date. The repository history preserves prior versions.

## Contact

For privacy questions that do not contain sensitive information, use the
[SDR Calibration Issues page](https://github.com/lbussy/SDR-Calibration/issues).
For sensitive security matters, use the private reporting process linked above.
